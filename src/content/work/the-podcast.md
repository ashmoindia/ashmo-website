## The Format

One person. One camera. No guests yet. This conversation format is built for people who want business judgment without performance.

Each episode picks a single idea and goes deep. Not motivational. Not instructional. Just honest thinking from someone who's been in the trenches.

## Why Audio and Video

Writing is where I shape my thinking. Some ideas are better heard than read: the pauses, the emphasis, and the judgment behind the words.

The podcast is on YouTube because video creates connection that audio alone can't. You can see when someone means what they're saying.

## What I Talk About

- Why brand positioning is harder than people think
- How customer behaviour shapes positioning and pricing
- The difference between patience and passivity
- How AI is changing commerce and what stays the same
- The real cost of building something that lasts

## Where It's Going

The conversations section is early. Episode one is live. The goal is not to pretend there is a large archive. The goal is to build a useful public record of business thinking over time.

Many of these ideas start as written pieces: [brand positioning](/thinking/brand-positioning-hardest-thing/), [campaign diagnosis](/thinking/most-campaigns-fail-before-the-creative-is-made/), and [patience as a strategy](/thinking/patience-is-not-passive/). Conversations give them more room.

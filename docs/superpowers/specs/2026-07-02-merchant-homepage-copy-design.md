# Merchant Homepage Copy Design

## Goal

Make the restored dark Ashmo homepage easier to understand for merchants, shopkeepers, small and medium business owners, and F&B operators.

## Approved Direction

Use a business-publication voice inspired by Forbes, Bloomberg, and Zawya: clear headlines, practical business language, and credible proof. The opening should be broad enough for merchants and shopkeepers, while the examples and proof should stay strongest in restaurants, cafes, and F&B.

## Audience

The primary reader is a busy owner or operator who wants more customers, better sales, clearer pricing, stronger marketing, and practical growth ideas. They may not understand phrases like "AI Marketing OS", "operator signal", "framework layers", or "commercial intelligence".

## Copy Rules

- Use plain business language over technical terms.
- Lead with customer, sales, pricing, marketing, repeat business, and growth.
- Keep F&B as the strongest credibility base.
- Preserve the restored dark `HeroExchange` homepage structure and dark-era navbar.
- Do not introduce fabricated claims, testimonials, or guarantees.
- Avoid making the site sound like a software product.

## Homepage Copy Changes

- Rewrite the hero headline away from "AI Marketing OS" and toward practical business growth.
- Rewrite the hero body for merchants, shopkeepers, SMB owners, and F&B operators.
- Rename the "AI Marketing OS" section into plain-language business help.
- Rewrite cards like "Signal capture" and "Operator review layer" into customer-facing business topics.
- Rewrite product/toolkit descriptions so they feel useful instead of consultant-heavy.
- Rewrite market intelligence sections using simple terms like "market notes" or "business briefings".
- Rewrite homepage title, default description, and structured-data descriptions so search/browser snippets match the plain-language direction.
- Keep CTA paths stable where possible, but change labels to plain action language.

## Success Criteria

- Homepage no longer uses "AI Marketing OS" in visible copy.
- Major above-the-fold copy can be understood by a non-technical merchant.
- Homepage still mentions restaurants, cafes, shops, and small businesses.
- Existing routes and SEO build checks continue to pass.
